Bigg Boss Stream - Custom Background Music Folder
===================================================

Drop your favorite background music tracks here!
Supported audio formats:
- .mp3
- .wav
- .m4a
- .aac
- .flac
- .ogg

How it works:
1. Copy any audio files (e.g. "lofi-beat.mp3", "bollywood-theme.mp3") into this folder.
2. When the stream starts (or restarts), the engine automatically decodes them with FFmpeg into high-fidelity PCM.
3. They are added at the top of the stream's playlist (e.g. Track 1, Track 2...).
4. You and your viewers can switch between them in live chat using:
   - !track 1
   - !track next
   - !track loop
   - !vol 80
